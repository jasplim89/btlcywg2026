Before you go — 3 quick questions

Here's your mirror ✨

There's no score and no ranking — you are not a number. These four are independent, and everyone moves back and forth depending on the day, the person, and the topic.

A gentle note: a lower score in a dimension doesn't make you a bad friend or a bad peer supporter. Holding space is a skill, like an instrument — nobody starts fluent, and even experienced supporters have off days. Retake this every couple of months and compare with yourself, not with others.

Share your results with the programme team?

This is completely voluntary and doesn't affect anything about your role. Your personal reflection and individual scenario answers are never collected — only your four dimension scores and what you fill in below.

📄 Data protection notice (Personal Data Protection Act 2012)

What we collect: your four dimension scores, your supporter style, your answers to the 3 quick questions, age, and — only if you choose to provide them — your name, contact details, and your interest in learning about self-support resources. We also record the date and time of submission. We do not collect your answers to individual questions or anything you wrote in your personal reflection.

Purposes: (1) to evaluate and improve the peer support programme, using scores in aggregated form; and (2) if you indicated interest, to contact you with information and resources on supporting your own wellbeing.

Storage & access: your data is stored securely within our organisation's Microsoft 365 environment and is accessible only to the programme team. It will not be sold, and will not be disclosed to third parties except as required by law. It will be retained only for as long as needed for the above purposes, and no longer than 2 years, after which it is deleted.

Your rights: providing personal data is voluntary. You may withdraw your consent, or request access to or correction of your data, at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at dpo@example.org. Withdrawing consent will not affect your participation in the programme in any way.

If you are under 18: please make sure you understand this notice before consenting — feel free to ask a facilitator, parent, or guardian to go through it with you first.
