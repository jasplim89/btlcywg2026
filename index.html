Here's your mirror ✨

There's no total score — you are not a number. These four dimensions are independent, and everyone moves back and forth depending on the day, the person, and the topic.

A gentle note: a lower score in a dimension doesn't make you a bad friend or a bad peer supporter. Holding space is a skill, like an instrument — nobody starts fluent, and even experienced supporters have off days. Retake this every couple of months and compare with yourself, not with others.

Help us make peer support training better?

You can share your four dimension scores anonymously with the programme team. We never collect your name, your answers to individual questions, or anything you wrote in Part 3.
